The app

Hearsay is free to download and use. It's provided as is, without warranty. Transcripts are machine-generated and can be wrong: check anything important against the recording.

Recording other people

Many places require everyone on a call to agree to being recorded. You are responsible for getting that consent. Hearsay shows a recording indicator in the menu bar but does not notify other participants for you.
