Questions

macOS says it can't check the app for malicious software

Hearsay isn't notarized by Apple yet. Open it once, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. You only need to do this the first time.

Do I need headphones?

For clean "Me" and "Them" tracks, yes. On speakers your mic picks up the other side too, so your track ends up containing both voices.

What happens if I lose my recovery code?

Backup is coming soon. When it launches: your backups can't be opened by anyone, including us. That's what end-to-end encryption means. The recordings on your Mac are unaffected, and you can see the code again in Hearsay on any Mac where backup is set up.

Is it legal to record calls?

It depends on where you and the other people are. Several US states and many countries require everyone's consent. When you start recording, Hearsay offers a short consent notice you can paste into the meeting chat in one click (also in the menu bar, and editable to your own wording). Asking is still your responsibility.

Intel Macs?

Not yet. Local transcription needs Apple Silicon to be fast enough.
