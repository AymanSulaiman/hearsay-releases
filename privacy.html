Privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Hearsay is built so that we have as little of your data as possible. This page lists all of it.

Without a backup subscription

We receive nothing. Recordings and transcripts are made and stored on your Mac. The app has no analytics or tracking. It makes two kinds of network request: downloading the speech model from Hugging Face the first time you transcribe, and, only if you allow it when first asked, a once-a-day check with GitHub for a newer version. That check sends nothing about you or your recordings; GitHub sees your IP address, as any website would. Updates never install without asking you.

With a backup subscription

Backup isn't available yet. This section describes how it will work when it launches.

  • Your email and a password hash, held by Google Firebase Authentication, so you can sign in.
  • Encrypted backups, held in Google Cloud Storage. Files are encrypted on your Mac with a key that only exists on your Macs. File names, folder names, audio and transcripts all arrive as ciphertext. We cannot read them, and we cannot recover them without your key.
  • Billing details, held by Stripe. We never see your card number.

Who processes data for us

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Storage), in the United States
  • Stripe (payments)
  • GitHub (hosts this website and the app downloads)

We don't sell data, show ads, or share data with anyone else.

Deleting your data

Cancelling stops new backups. To delete your account and every backup, contact us and we'll do it within 30 days. Recordings on your Mac are yours and are never touched.
